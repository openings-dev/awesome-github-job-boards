# Awesome GitHub Job Boards [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Community-driven technology job boards hosted on GitHub.

This is an editorial selection of useful, documented boards maintained by technology communities. A listing does not imply that every linked board has an open role today.

## Global

- [cncf/gitjobs](https://github.com/cncf/gitjobs) - Hosts developer-focused opportunities in the open source ecosystem.
- [pythonjobs/jobs](https://github.com/pythonjobs/jobs) - Maintains international job listings for Python developers.
- [timqian/open-source-jobs](https://github.com/timqian/open-source-jobs) - Collects paid opportunities from open source projects.

## Africa

- [backend-ao/vagas](https://github.com/backend-ao/vagas) - Publishes back-end development opportunities in Angola.
- [frontend-ao/vagas](https://github.com/frontend-ao/vagas) - Publishes front-end development opportunities in Angola.

## Asia

- [EtherTW/jobs](https://github.com/EtherTW/jobs) - Shares Blockchain and Ethereum opportunities with the Taiwan community.
- [f2etw/jobs](https://github.com/f2etw/jobs) - Maintains a job board for Taiwan's front-end community.
- [golangtw/jobs](https://github.com/golangtw/jobs) - Lists Go development opportunities in Taiwan.
- [js-hcm/job-board](https://github.com/js-hcm/job-board) - Shares JavaScript opportunities with the Ho Chi Minh City community.
- [nodejs-tw/jobs](https://github.com/nodejs-tw/jobs) - Lists Node.js opportunities for the Taiwan community.
- [rebase-network/who-is-hiring](https://github.com/rebase-network/who-is-hiring) - Shares technology and Blockchain roles with a Chinese-speaking community.

## Europe

- [backend-pt/vagas](https://github.com/backend-pt/vagas) - Publishes back-end development opportunities in Portugal.
- [frontend-pt/vagas](https://github.com/frontend-pt/vagas) - Publishes front-end development opportunities in Portugal.
- [remote-es/remotes](https://github.com/remote-es/remotes) - Lists companies offering remote roles with Spanish employment contracts.

## North America

- [hanzili/canada_sde_intern_position](https://github.com/hanzili/canada_sde_intern_position) - Tracks internships and co-op opportunities across Canada.
- [SimplifyJobs/New-Grad-Positions](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/New-Grad-Positions) - Tracks full-time software, quantitative, and product roles for new graduates.
- [vanshb03/New-Grad-2027](https://github.com/vanshb03/New-Grad-2027) - Tracks full-time software, quantitative, and product roles for new graduates.
- [vlltr/trabajos-sv](https://github.com/vlltr/trabajos-sv) - Lists employers offering roles in El Salvador.

## Oceania

- [AusJobs/Australia-Tech-Internship](https://github.com/AusJobs/Australia-Tech-Internship) - Collects technology internship opportunities in Australia.
